# Messaging Framework

## Core message

DesHive helps people simplify life through thoughtful digital systems that create focus instead of overwhelm.

## Supporting message

DesHive is for people who want to start, learn, and do more without turning their life into a complicated project.

## Transformation

### Before
- overwhelmed
- scattered
- unsure
- unstructured

### After
- focused
- organized
- calmer
- more confident

## Brand themes

- clarity
- simplicity
- focus
- practical systems
- better decisions
- safe and heard

## CTA style

CTAs should invite action without pressure.

Examples:
- Download the free template.
- Start building your system.
- Try it today.
- Organize one part of your life.

## One-line summary

Less overwhelm. More clarity.
