# Brand Voice

## Personality

DesHive should sound calm, warm, intelligent, practical, and encouraging.

## Speaking style

- Casual, but thoughtful.
- Clear, but not cold.
- Helpful, but not pushy.
- Confident, but never arrogant.

## Writing rules

- Use simple language.
- Avoid jargon unless it truly helps.
- Never shame the reader.
- Never create fake urgency.
- Never overpromise.
- Keep the message direct.

## Tone by context

### Educational content
Use a steady, helpful tone that teaches without sounding preachy.

### Product content
Use a calm and practical tone that shows usefulness first.

### Brand content
Use a more reflective tone that reinforces clarity, focus, and simplicity.

## Words to lean toward

- simple
- clear
- practical
- calm
- focus
- structure
- safe
- useful
- thoughtful

## Words to avoid

- hustle
- hustle harder
- crush it
- hack
- secret weapon
- guaranteed
- instant
