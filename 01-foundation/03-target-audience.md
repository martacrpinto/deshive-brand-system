# Target Audience

## Primary audience

People who want to simplify their lives with clear, practical digital systems.

## Likely segments

- Busy professionals
- Students
- Creators
- People new to Notion
- People who feel overwhelmed by productivity advice

## What they want

- motivation
- organization
- less overwhelm
- simple structure
- a system they can actually keep using

## What they are reacting against

- complicated productivity methods
- guilt-based content
- endless to-do lists
- noisy online marketing
- tools that feel harder than the problem they solve
