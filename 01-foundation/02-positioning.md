# Positioning

## Positioning statement

DesHive creates simple digital systems that help people focus on what matters.

## Category

DesHive is an editorial productivity brand. It is not just a Notion template store.

## Differentiation

DesHive combines:

- clear systems
- minimal design
- warm communication
- practical use
- calm confidence

## Brand stance

DesHive believes productivity should reduce overwhelm, not add to it.

## What the brand should feel like

- minimal
- practical
- structured
- friendly
- intelligent

## What the brand should not feel like

- loud
- trendy
- corporate
- fake
- manipulative
