# Illustration System

## Women
Store approved illustrations in:
assets/illustrations/women/

## Men
Store approved illustrations in:
assets/illustrations/men/

## Style
- Black outline only
- No fills
- No gradients
- Halftone clothing allowed
- Transparent background
- Editorial line art
- Cropped by the frame

## Not Allowed
- Anime
- 3D
- Realistic portraits
- Multiple characters
